PUTTING THE PORTFOLIO ONLINE

1. Buy a domain (Namecheap, Porkbun or GoDaddy), e.g. gabrielolaleye.com
2. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN with your real domain in these three files:
   index.html, robots.txt, sitemap.xml
   (Mac/Linux: sed -i 's/YOUR-DOMAIN/gabrielolaleye.com/g' index.html robots.txt sitemap.xml
    Windows: open each file in Notepad, then Find and Replace.)
3. Host it free: drag this whole folder into Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop)
   or Cloudflare Pages. Then connect your domain in the site's Domain settings.
4. Google: add your site at search.google.com/search-console, submit
   https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml and use Request Indexing on the homepage.
5. Link to the site from Instagram, LinkedIn and your Gabex Designs profiles.

Folder: index.html (site), img/ (designs), og-image.jpg (link preview), robots.txt, sitemap.xml
