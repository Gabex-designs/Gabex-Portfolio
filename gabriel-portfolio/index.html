How we work together

A clear path so you always know what happens next and when you will see something.

  1. Brief

    You tell me about the business, the audience and what the design must do. I ask until it is clear.

  2. Direction

    I share a colour, type and layout direction before designing everything, so we agree early.

  3. Design

    I build the work and take your feedback in clear rounds until it is right.

  4. Handover

    You receive the final files in the sizes you need, ready to post, print or hand to your team.
